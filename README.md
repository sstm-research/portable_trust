# Portable Trust

**From Digital Product Passports to Creator Rights**

Portable Trust is an open research initiative investigating how physical
artworks and creators can carry verifiable identity, provenance, rights
and lifecycle evidence across organizations and platforms.

> How can a physical artwork carry its verifiable history between
> artist, gallery and collectors without depending on a single
> platform as the authority?

## Core principle

The platform does not own the truth.

Independent actors issue evidence.
Portable Trust makes that evidence understandable,
verifiable and portable.

## Architecture

ISSUER
↓
CREDENTIAL / ATTESTATION
↓
HOLDER
↓
VERIFIER

Portable Trust investigates the interoperability of:

- institutional identity
- ICP-Brasil
- ENS
- Verifiable Credentials
- Ethereum Attestation Service
- Ethereum
- Artwork IDs
- NFC / QR
- UNTP / UNECE
- creator-rights infrastructure
- AI Rights Agents
- anti-capture governance

## Governance

Portable Trust explores infrastructure that resists both:

**platform capture**
and
**governance capture**

Anti-Capture / Blockful is used as a governance research reference.

## Standards

UNTP / UNECE provides an interoperability reference for:

product identity → credentials → lifecycle events → verification.

Portable Trust investigates how this pattern can extend from
products to artworks, creators and rights.
